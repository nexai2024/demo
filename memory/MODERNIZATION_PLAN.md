# Flowcast — Modernization Plan (30 items)

Legend: Effort / Impact = Low · Medium · High. Status: ✅ implemented in this iteration · ⏳ deferred (roadmap)

## Part 1 — 15 High-Impact Enhancements to Existing Features

| # | Title | Category | Why (bottleneck / pain) | How (implementation) | Effort / Impact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Route-level code splitting | Performance / Bundle | Studio, player and settings ship in one bundle; public share/embed viewers download the whole editor (dialogs, cmdk, reorder) just to watch a demo. | `React.lazy` + `Suspense` per route; the embed route imports only `DemoPlayer`. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 2 | SSE run streaming instead of 1 s polling | Performance / Realtime | Polling every second spends a full request per tick per viewer and adds up to 1 s of delay on "Step 3/6…" updates. | `GET /api/runs/{id}/stream` (`text/event-stream`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`) diffs the run doc and pushes it; `useRun` hook uses `EventSource(withCredentials)` and drops back to polling if SSE errors. | Medium / High | ✅ |
| 3 | Thumbnails + lazy media | Performance / Rendering | The timeline renders full 1280×800 JPEGs (~150 KB each) in 15 cards, which hurts LCP and memory. | Pillow makes a 480px WebP `thumb_path` per capture; timeline/dashboard use `<img loading="lazy" decoding="async">` on the thumb, and only the player loads the full frame. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 4 | Debounced autosave + dirty guard | State / UX | Edits can be lost if the user forgets "Save", navigates away, or records with unsaved steps. | 1.2 s debounced PUT, a "Saving… / Saved" indicator, a `beforeunload` guard while dirty, and a forced flush before any record/voiceover/export call. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 5 | Undo / redo for the timeline | State management | One wrong delete or AI regeneration destroys hand-tuned captions. | `useUndoable` hook (past/present/future stacks, capped at 50) with ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z and toolbar buttons; server snapshots in History cover everything older. | Medium / High | ✅ |
| 6 | Drag-to-reorder with keyboard fallback | UX / A11y | Moving steps up/down one click at a time is slow, and drag-only lists exclude keyboard users. | framer-motion `Reorder.Group` with a drag handle, plus labelled up/down buttons (aria-label) for keyboard and screen-reader users. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 7 | Accessible player & live console | Accessibility | Screen readers miss automation progress, and the player is mouse-only. | Console is `role="log" aria-live="polite"`; player handles Space/←/→/M keys, has a focus-visible ring and aria-labels on all controls, and honours `prefers-reduced-motion`. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 8 | Self-healing selectors | Reliability | One brittle selector fails a whole recording, the most common failure mode. | AI returns `fallback_selectors[]` per step; the engine tries the primary, then each fallback (with retries); the selector that worked is saved back as `resolved_selector`. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 9 | Job cancellation | UX / Cost | A runaway recording keeps a paid Browserless session open and locks the project. | `POST /api/runs/{id}/cancel` sets a flag; the progress callback raises `Cancelled` between steps, the browser closes in `finally`, and the run is marked `cancelled`. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 10 | LLM output validation + repair retry | Reliability / AI | Claude sometimes returns truncated or fenced JSON, and the user sees a raw 502. | Pydantic validation of every step (invalid ones dropped); on a parse failure, one automatic "return valid JSON only" repair turn in the same chat session. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 11 | Export fingerprint cache | Performance / Cost | Exporting the same unchanged MP4 twice repeats about 30 s of ffmpeg work. | SHA-1 of the render inputs (steps, narration, brand); if an export with the same fingerprint and format exists, it is returned immediately. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 12 | Lean project list payload | Performance | The dashboard downloaded every step of every project just to show a count. | Mongo aggregation `$project` with `step_count: {$size: "$steps"}`, with the steps and draft fields left out. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 13 | Sensitive-input masking | Security / UX | Recording login flows shows passwords in the timeline UI and could leak them. | Per-step `sensitive` flag: masked input in the editor, typed text never sent to public endpoints, and the AI flags password fields automatically. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 14 | Offline / network-loss banner | Edge-case UX | Autosave silently fails on flaky Wi-Fi, so users think work was saved. | `useOnline` hook (`online`/`offline` events) shows a sticky banner and pauses autosave; queued edits flush when the connection returns. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 15 | Player media preloading | Rendering / UX | Frames and voiceover visibly pop in on each step change, especially on mobile. | Preload the next step's image (`new Image()`) and audio (`<link rel=preload>` style `Audio()` warm-up) while the current step plays. | Low / Medium | ✅ |

## Part 2 — 15 Net-New Features

| # | Title | Category | Why (value) | How (implementation) | Effort / Impact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Fix with AI" selector repair | AI / Workflow | Failed steps currently need DevTools skills to fix. | `POST /steps/{id}/repair`: Browserless inspects the step's last page URL, and Claude gets the failed selector, error and live element list, then returns a new selector plus fallbacks applied in one click. | Medium / High | ✅ |
| 2 | AI narration rewrite (tone presets) | AI / Value | Writing captions and voiceover for 12 steps by hand is the slowest part of making a demo. | `POST /projects/{id}/rewrite {instruction}` with presets (Concise, Friendly, Technical, Sales-y); Claude rewrites the caption/voiceover pairs in one JSON round-trip and the result is undoable. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 3 | AI translation of narration | AI / Reach | Global launches need localized demos. | Same rewrite pipeline with "Translate to {language}" presets; TTS regenerates only the changed clips (audio_text diff). | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 4 | Viewer analytics & drop-off funnel | Retention / Value | Creators can't tell whether a shared demo was watched or where viewers left. | Public player beacons `view/step/complete` to `POST /public/{share}/events`; Studio shows views, completion rate and a per-step reach bar chart. | Medium / High | ✅ |
| 5 | Brand kit (accent, caption position, watermark) | Monetization / Value | Generic-looking demos don't match the customer's brand. | `project.brand` {accent, caption_position, watermark}, applied in the player via CSS variables and burned into GIF/MP4 frames by Pillow. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 6 | Template gallery | Activation | A blank draft box stops new users; time-to-first-demo is the main activation metric. | NewProjectDialog lists curated, working templates (TodoMVC tour, login flow) that prefill name, URL and draft. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 7 | Command palette (⌘K) | Modern UI | Power users want keyboard-first control of record/export/share. | `cmdk` dialog in the Studio with actions (Generate, Record, Voiceover, Export, Add step, Copy share link, History). | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 8 | Deep-linkable chapters in player | UX / Sharing | Support teams want to link to step 4, not the whole demo. | Chapter list built from captions; `?step=N` in the share/embed URL; "copy link to this step" button. | Low / Medium | ✅ |
| 9 | Usage meter & plan limits | Monetization | No cost guardrail on Browserless/LLM spend, and no natural upgrade moment. | Count `record` runs per calendar month per user; Settings shows a progress meter; recording returns 402 with an upgrade hint above the plan limit. | Medium / High | ✅ |
| 10 | Live capture filmstrip | Micro-interaction | During recording the user stares at a log; showing frames as they arrive is the "aha" moment. | Run updates trigger project refetch; new thumbs animate into the filmstrip with framer-motion, and the player follows the latest captured step. | Low / High | ✅ |
| 11 | Scheduled re-recording | Automation / Retention | UIs change; demos go stale without anyone noticing. | Platform cron (`.emergent/crons.yml`) hits a secured webhook weekly, re-records projects with `auto_refresh`, and flags failed steps. | Medium / High | ⏳ |
| 12 | Slack / email notifications | Workflow | Long exports finish while the user is in another tab. | Webhook URL per workspace; post on run completion/failure with thumbnail and links. | Low / Medium | ⏳ |
| 13 | Password-protected & expiring share links | Security / Enterprise | Pre-launch features can't be shared publicly. | Optional bcrypt-hashed share password + `expires_at`; public endpoints need a short-lived viewer token. | Medium / Medium | ⏳ |
| 14 | Team workspaces & comments | Collaboration | Demos are reviewed by PMs and marketing, not just their creator. | `workspaces` collection with roles; per-step comment threads; activity feed. | High / High | ⏳ |
| 15 | Paid plans checkout | Monetization | Usage limits need an upgrade path. | Stripe checkout for Pro tier lifting the monthly recording limit and watermark. | Medium / High | ⏳ |
